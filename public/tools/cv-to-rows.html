How it works. The page reads the text layer of each PDF and pulls out contact details, current role, company, experience, education, skills, notice period and CTC using pattern rules.

What to expect. Email, phone and LinkedIn are reliable. Role, company and experience are best-effort and flagged under Review when unsure. Two-column or heavily designed CVs parse less cleanly. Scanned CVs need OCR first.

Size. This tab handles batches of a few hundred to a few thousand CVs. For a full 10,000-file dump, run the same extraction as a script.

Next step. The Excel file includes blank Verification status, Last verified and Consent to contact columns, ready for the WhatsApp or Telegram confirmation round.
